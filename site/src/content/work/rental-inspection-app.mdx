<Chapter n="01" title="What we found">

At the end of a rental, tenants and landlords often disagree about the condition of the property. Without proof either way, the deposit turns into an argument.

The problem wasn't the inspection itself. It was that nobody trusted a record the other side could have changed.

</Chapter>

<Chapter n="02" title="What we built">

An app for tenants and property owners in European countries, on iOS, Android and the web.

1. **Move-in walk-through.** The tenant goes room by room, taking photos as they go.
2. **A sealed record.** Every photo goes into a tamper-proof record that neither side can edit later.
3. **Move-out walk-through.** At the end of the tenancy they walk the same rooms again.
4. **Side by side.** Both sides compare the move-in and move-out condition of each room in one view.

</Chapter>

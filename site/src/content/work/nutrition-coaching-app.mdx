<Chapter n="01" title="What we found">

People who track their meals usually have nobody to ask what the numbers mean. Generic diet apps answer with generic advice, and most people stop logging within weeks.

</Chapter>

<Chapter n="02" title="What we built">

A mobile app where people log meals and track their nutrition, with a human side built in.

1. **Meal logging and tracking.** Quick to log, with nutrition tracked over time.
2. **A real nutritionist inside the app.** Users talk directly to a nutrition doctor, in the same place they track what they eat.
3. **On every screen.** The same product runs on iOS, Android and the web.

</Chapter>

<Chapter n="03" title="What changed">

The app is live, and we still market and maintain it: new releases, fixes and the campaigns that bring new users in.

</Chapter>

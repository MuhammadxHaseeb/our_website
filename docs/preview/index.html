1 · Epox, Medium

Title case, Medium weight, tracking −4%. The calmest and most "established". Reads like a company name, not a startup.

Header size, 22px

2 · epox, Bold lowercase

Lowercase, Bold, tight −6.5%. Compact and rounded: the best shape for the liquid-metal animation (thick strokes, blob-friendly "e" and "o").

Header size, 22px

3 · EPOX, Light caps

Capitals, Light, open +6%. Most "luxury/editorial". Thin strokes look elegant large but get fragile small and in liquid metal.

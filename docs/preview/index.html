Epox: palettes & fonts in real UI

Step 1 preview for the design brief (docs/design-brief.md). Each column is the same mini page (hero, content section, card, buttons, footer) in one palette. Use the controls to switch the hero between dark and light and to try every font option. Contrast for every text/background pair is computed live below each column (WCAG AA: 4.5:1 body, 3:1 large text). Hover the buttons and links to feel the 100ms Linear-style feedback.

1 · Palettes

Black and white do ~90% of the work in all three. The third colour is used only for emphasis: one word in the hero, the "live" status dot, the primary-button hover, the footer accent. Silver (A) is never used as text on white (it fails contrast); on light backgrounds A uses its darker steel tone.

2 · Fonts: free alternatives next to the originals

Left: crop of the original font from research/raw/. Right: two free candidates per role (+ one alternate), set with our own words at similar size. All are free: Google Fonts (Inter, Inter Tight, Geist, Geist Mono, JetBrains Mono, IBM Plex Mono, Fraunces, Instrument Serif) or Fontshare (Switzer, Zodiak).

# Notes from the studio.

Working things out in the open. Mostly arguments we’ve already had internally.

- Good enough became the standard Somewhere along the way the bare minimum got rebranded as pragmatism. Most things you touch every day are worse than they need to be, and everyone who shipped them knows it. That gap is where we started, and it hasn’t narrowed much. Read

- The feature is not the point Voice-to-text is a feature. Closing the distance between having a thought and having it written down is a change in how someone works. Only one of those is worth building. Ask what actually changes for a person, then go find the thing underneath that makes it happen. Read

- Most bad answers are good answers to the wrong question You can spend an enormous amount of compute answering something precisely and still be useless. The expensive part was never the answer. It was noticing the question was wrong before you committed to it. Read

- You should be able to check our work Any system that tells you something true and won’t show you why is asking for trust it hasn’t earned. Sources aren’t a compliance feature bolted on at the end. If you can’t verify it, we haven’t finished. Read

- Direction beats capability Technology keeps handing you more things you could do. It has never once told you which of them is worth doing. That judgement is the scarce part, and it gets scarcer as the doing gets cheaper. Read

# Mediocrity got normalised. We didn’t agree.

Notiune means notion

Notiune is a design and engineering studio. Three people, client work and six products of our own, nobody in between us and the work. That’s the whole description.

## The standard

We don’t ask whether it’s good enough to hand over. We ask whether we’d still choose it a month later. That’s a harder question, and it’s the reason things take longer here.

## How we work

- Sharp A brief usually arrives as a solution wearing a problem’s clothes. We take it apart first. That is slower on day one and faster by the end of the month.

- Built, not presented Thinking is supposed to produce movement. We would rather build the answer than write another deck about the problem.

- Detail-obsessed Typography, spacing, motion, load time, the words themselves. None of it is small, especially the parts nobody consciously notices, which are usually the reason something feels right.

## What we won’t trade

Soul.

Something can be fast, accessible and perfectly aligned and still feel like nobody was home when it was made. The details that give it a pulse are the cheapest ones to cut, and the only ones anyone remembers.

## The line we hold to

> “We are what we repeatedly do. Excellence, therefore, is not an act but a habit.”

Will Durant, summarising Aristotle

Not a slogan for a wall. It’s a description of how work actually gets made, or doesn’t. Excellence that only shows up when someone is watching isn’t excellence, it’s performance.

Our own products

## Six products, live and in people’s hands.

The feature is never the interesting part, so here’s what sits underneath each one.

- Tiune Voice-to-text for macOS and Windows. Hold a key, talk, let go. The finished text is already at your cursor, punctuated, false starts gone, in whichever languages you just mixed. Most dictation tools before it asked the person to change: speak slower, speak flatter, say the word “period” out loud. People correctly refused. So the work happens on our side of the microphone, and you get to talk the way you already do. (opens in a new tab)

- OptimismAI A decision tool that rewrites your question before it answers it. Most bad answers are perfectly good answers to the wrong question. Fix the question and the rest usually takes care of itself. (opens in a new tab)

- Ask Richard Conversational AI built on the voice and perspective of Richard Polk, who has run Pedestrian Shops in Boulder for five decades. Not a chatbot wearing someone’s name. We were after the actual shape of how he thinks. That is harder to get right, and very obvious when you don’t. (opens in a new tab)

- Ask the Founders Modern questions answered from the writings of the founding generation, every claim carrying its source. The sources aren’t a footnote, they’re the product. Without them it’s just another confident voice, and there is no shortage of those. (opens in a new tab)

- IsItTrue Claims scored against tiered independent sources before any model is allowed an opinion. Everyone calls it fact-checking. It’s really about epistemic uncertainty: not whether something got flagged, but how much you should trust what you just read. (opens in a new tab)

- Boulder Bubble A news outlet covering current events and international relations: what happened, and which forces made it happen. Spectacle is the cheapest thing to produce and the fastest to forget. The bet is on context: you finish a piece and read next week’s news better than you did last week’s. (opens in a new tab)

All six are ours. Tiune ships under our own name, and the other five under The Optimism Company (opens in a new tab) .

# Dream With AI

As philosophers, neuroscientists, and engineers debate whether AI will ever possess genuine sentience, we decided to ask a different question: If an AI could feel, what would it dream? Dream with AI explores that premise by giving it room to unfold. Our team created an AI experience that autonomously generates a new world each night, complete with its own visuals and soundscapes. It’s an imaginative exploration of what AI might dream if it could feel emotion, inviting you to spend a little time with that possibility… and see how it changes every day.

### Autonomous Dreams

Every midnight, an AI dreams a new 3D world. No one prompts or checks it. Automated tests verify it, then it goes live. One new dream a night.

### Eye Tracking

Your face is the controller. Blink to move on. Close your eyes to wake somewhere new. Turn and lean to explore. Nothing is recorded.

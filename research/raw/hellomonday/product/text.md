# A booster rocket for digital product teams

Our journey into digital product innovation started 10 years ago, when we helped Google improve the Hangouts experience – now Meet.

Since then we’ve collaborated with startups and product departments around the world to invent and reinvent the products of tomorrow.

We thrive equally when turning a napkin sketch into a Minimum Loveable Product, as we do exploring new product ideas and improvements. Always with a human touch and a bit of magic.

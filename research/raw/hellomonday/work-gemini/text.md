# Gemini - AI for Every Developer

When the AI sector exploded with new possibilities, and developers were navigating a complex ecosystem. Google, with its vast array of AI tools—APIs, open models, and on-device options—needed to cut through the noise. We took on the challenge of transforming ai.google.dev into a compelling hub, crafting a user experience that not only highlighted Google's offerings but also empowered developers to understand their potential and choose Google as their go-to AI partner.

### Look and Feel

The dark background, infused with dynamic blue tones, created a sense of depth and technological prowess. These striking visuals, coupled with precisely calibrated pops of color, drew attention to key features and functionalities, reflecting Google's leadership in the AI space.

### User Flow

We structured the website with intuitive pathways and clear signposting, ensuring that every element was readily discoverable. This user-centric design empowered developers to quickly grasp the breadth of Google's AI offerings and immediately begin building their applications.

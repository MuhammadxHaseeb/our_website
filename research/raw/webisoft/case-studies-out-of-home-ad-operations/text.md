A NATIONAL OUT-OF-HOME ADVERTISING NETWORK

# One system for every billboard campaign, every team and every permission.

A central platform for out-of-home advertising operations, with complex admin workflows and fine-grained access control.

01 / THE CHALLENGE

Selling and running out-of-home campaigns means many teams touching the same work. The admin workflows were complex, and each person needed to see and change only what their role allowed.

02 / WHAT WE BUILT

- B001 Process analysis We mapped the business processes in depth and digitized the critical workflows.

- B002 UX and UI design An interface designed around how the operations teams actually work.

- B003 Backend The platform core that coordinates many users on the same campaigns.

- B004 Role-based access Granular permissions that protect the integrity and security of every operation.

03 / THE RESULT

One central system for advertising operations, with each user limited to what their role allows.

# An ERP and a SaaS product that keep running while the business changes.

Ongoing development and maintenance of an ERP estate and a B2B2B SaaS product, with our engineers working inside the client team.

Let's talk

01 / THE CHALLENGE

The business runs on a core operational system surrounded by apps and integrations. They have to keep working correctly every day, while new features keep shipping. In this business an error is not an inconvenience. It costs money.

02 / WHAT WE BUILT

- B001 ERP development Continuous development on the ERP the operation runs on.

- B002 A B2B2B SaaS product Building and maintaining a SaaS product the group sells to its own business customers.

- B003 Engineers inside the team Our engineers work in the client’s environment and processes, not at arm’s length.

- B004 Maintenance and new work together One team keeps what exists healthy and ships what is next.

03 / THE RESULT

More than four years in, the same team still builds and maintains both systems. The engagement is active.

## What it is
- Blink Co Technologies: a **restaurant management system (RMS)** for restaurants and cafes, also supermarkets, grocers and other retail.
- Five products: POS, online ordering (branded website + app), delivery fleet management, loyalty & engagement, data & analytics.
- Markets: Pakistan (Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad), Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Bahrain, Kuwait, South Africa. Offices Karachi, Lahore, Riyadh.
- History: 2018 aggregator app → 2020 direct online ordering → 2022 fleet → 2023 Saudi → 2024 POS.
- Claims: 8M+ orders a year, 2000+ POS deployments in Pakistan, "3000+ brands". A "real results" stats block on their site shows unfilled zeros.

## Products and features
**1. POS / RMS**
- Cloud POS usable from anywhere, offline mode when the connection drops.
- One order hub: counter + own website/app + Foodpanda orders.
- Menu management, open / on-hold orders, inventory linked to recipes, food costing, warehouse management (top tier).
- Revenue reconciliation across channels; kitchen tickets printed per category and station.
- Reports, discounts, shift schedule; tax integrations.

**2. Online ordering**
- Branded website and mobile apps (Flutter), own or built-in payment gateway.
- Menu management, geo-fencing (order goes to the nearest branch), location-based menus, real-time tracking.
- CRM with segmentation, BI reports, multilingual, account manager.
- Supermarket variant: up to 100,000 SKUs, multi-level product hierarchy, category filters.

**3. Fleet / rider management**
- Rider app: auto / manual assignment, optimised routes, rejection reasons.
- Rider portal: live tracking, bulk task upload, reports (performance, attendance, assignment logs), dashboard (status, distance, completion times).
- No own riders → orders go to logistics partners (Bykea, pandago).

**4. Loyalty & engagement**
- Promo codes, points, e-wallet, discount engine.
- Automatic SMS / email / push on sign-up, order, birthday, abandoned cart.

**5. Data & analytics**
- Sales by city / branch / category; retention (repeat rate, lifetime value, churn).
- Conversion, basket analysis, product analytics, rejections, reviews, delivery times.

**Integrations named:** Foodpanda, pandago, Savyour, Bykea, tax authorities, payment gateways, delivery providers, POS middleware.
